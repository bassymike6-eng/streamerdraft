# StreamerDraft: launch and marketing plan

**The goal:** 300 people confirmed (streamers and scouts) by the close,
**23:59 on Monday 16 November 2026**. That unlocks GTA 6 (out 19 November)
for the top referrer. Under 300, the top referrer gets £25.

**The real goal behind it:** small UK streamers and creators who'll actually
use StreamerDraft when the season starts. The 300 gets attention. The
streamers are the product.

## 2. The live test (Wednesday, on your phone)

Use real emails you can open (yours, a family member's).

- [ ] The home page loads on phone and laptop. Counter shows **0**
      (delete any test sign-ups from the live database first)
- [ ] Join as a streamer → the email arrives (not in spam) → confirm → read
      the rules → tick → you get your #number and referral link
- [ ] Open that referral link on another phone → join as a **scout** →
      confirm → the counter goes up, and the first person's page shows
      1 counted referral
- [ ] `/admin` works with the NEW password. The scout shows under Scouts.
      "Looks good" works. Turn down + Undo works
- [ ] Send yourself a test email from the dashboard (Write an email → Send test)
- [ ] An unsubscribe link works
- [ ] `/rules` shows your name, address and contact email (no "[your name]")

**Family feedback:** let them sign up for real and watch them do it without
helping. Wherever they hesitate or ask a question is what to fix. Only fix
what's actually confusing or broken: launch beats perfect.

## 3. Launch night (Thu 22 Oct)

**Before (afternoon):**
- Story on IG and Snapchat (template below): "first stream tonight, 6pm,
  announcement you don't want to miss".
- DM friends: the project + the stream in one message (template below).
- Set the Twitch schedule (Thursday 6pm) and title, e.g.
  *"FIRST EVER STREAM + big announcement 🔴"*.
- StreamElements: `!draft` command and the 15-minute timer are on.

**On stream:**
- 0:00–0:05: Loading scene with music while people come in.
- First hour: vibe, chat, games. Say hi to everyone by name.
- **Around 45–60 minutes in** (when the most people are watching): the
  announcement. Show the site on screen, explain it in 30 seconds: what
  it is, the Road to 300, GTA 6 for whoever refers the most. Type the link
  in chat and **pin it**.
- Mention it again once in the second hour (new people arrive). Don't
  repeat it every 10 minutes: the timer does that.
- End: "Stream ended" scene, thank people.

**The 30-second pitch:**
> "I built something. It's called StreamerDraft. If you're a small
> streamer, you sign up, keep streaming like normal, and bigger creators
> scout you and help you grow for a whole season. If you're a creator, you
> can be a scout. And to launch it: when you sign up you get your own link.
> Whoever gets the most people to sign up wins GTA 6 if we hit 300 by the
> 16th of November. It's free. Link's in chat."

## 4. After launch: the daily rhythm

**Stream days** (aim for 2–3 a week, same time, 6pm):
- Story before going live, mention StreamerDraft once on stream, thank-you
  post after with the link.
- Give the Road to 300 number on stream ("we're at 84, let's go").

**Days off stream** (20–30 minutes a day):
- Post one clip from the last stream (TikTok, Reels, Shorts). Add the link
  in the bio and a line in the caption.
- One update story: the counter, a shout-out to people who joined.
- **10 DMs to small UK streamers or creators** (the outreach template
  below). These are the people StreamerDraft is for.
- Check the dashboard: sign-ups today, referrals, any flags.

**Weekly:** send one email to the list from the dashboard (where the counter
is, what's coming, the link). Keep the Discord alive with the same updates.

## 5. Word of mouth or reaching new people?

**Both, from day one, but weighted.**

- **Word of mouth gets the count up.** The competition is built for it:
  everyone who joins gets a link and a reason to share it. But it needs
  seeds. Followers who see a story mostly don't act: expect a small share
  of story viewers to sign up. The ones who matter are the few who go hard
  for GTA 6 and bring 20 people each.
- **Outreach finds the right people.** Friends signing up helps the 300,
  but most aren't streamers. Small UK streamers are who StreamerDraft is
  built for, and they won't hear about it from your friends. So the 10 DMs
  a day aren't a backup plan: they're the real product work.

**The checkpoints** (look at the dashboard each Thursday):

| Checkpoint | On track | If you're behind |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sun 25 Oct (day 3) | 30+ | Message close friends personally, ask them to share their link |
| Thu 29 Oct (day 7) | 80+ | Go up to 25 DMs a day; post in UK small-streamer Discords (follow their self-promo rules) |
| Thu 5 Nov (day 14) | 170+ | Ask a few scouts and streamers who joined to do a shout-out; consider a small creator collab |
| Thu 12 Nov (day 21) | 250+ | Final push: "4 days left, 50 to go" on stream, stories and email |

To hit 300 in 25 days, you need about **12 a day**. The first two days will
be your biggest. The middle is where it slows down, so that's where the
outreach matters.

## 7. Things to watch

- **Email limit:** the free Resend plan sends 100 emails a day. Every
  sign-up is one email. If a day gets close to 100, upgrade for that week,
  or some people won't get their confirm link.
- **Cheating:** before announcing the winner, check #1 on the admin
  leaderboard for red flags (same IP, bursts of sign-ups). Disqualify if
  needed: #2 moves up.
- **Stay honest in posts:** GTA 6 is only for the top referrer, and only
  at 300. Under 300 it's £25. Say "if we hit 300".
- **Platform rules:** the competition says it isn't run by Twitch,
  Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat or X (it's in the rules). Don't make people
  follow, like or share as a condition of entering.
